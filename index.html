About

I'm a full-stack developer who ends up running the thing I built, which turns out to be a different job than writing the code.

Most of my work sits where software meets a business decision. I founded ReEnvision and taught its curriculum to more than a hundred students. I'm CTO at VYTL, a virtual enterprise company, where the technical calls and the commercial ones land on the same desk. And I built MoonlightV because I was tired of running my toolchain across five tabs and two machines.

The interests that keep showing up are business administration, fintech, AI, and full-stack development — and I'm most useful where they overlap. Winning a stock trading competition and getting a 968 on a small business certification are the same curiosity as wiring five AI providers into one interface: I want to know how the system actually works, then build the thing that uses it.

My bias is toward fewer moving parts and getting something in front of a real person early. A lot of what I build has no build step at all — you open the file and it works — because the things that survive are the ones someone else can pick up without a manual. I'd rather finish six things properly than start twenty.
