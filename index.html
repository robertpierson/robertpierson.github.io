About

I'm the technical one and the one who runs it. Full-stack means I write the frontend, the API, and the schema — but the part I actually like is everything after that: what to charge, who it's for, why the person who signed up on Tuesday never came back.

My bias is toward fewer moving parts and shipping to a real person early. A lot of what I build has no build step at all — open the file and it works — because the things that survive are the ones anyone can pick up without a manual. I'd rather have three finished products than ten repositories.

The one I care most about is ReEnvision. Turning 96 lessons into something a teacher can actually run, on a phone, with no connection, turned out to be the real work. The code was the cheap part. That's been true of every project on this page, and it's most of why I want to do this full time.
